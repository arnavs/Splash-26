# Labyrinthian (Bromjunaar): Reuse Across Eras

*Optional coda after Bleak Falls, 2–5 min from screenshots. Too long and too gated to play live.*

Facts and quotes below are checked against UESP. Anything UESP doesn't confirm is marked **(unverified)**.

**Why this site:** Bleak Falls has only two layers (Dragon Cult, then squatters). Labyrinthian is the deck's best example of **one site reused across many eras**. It also pays off the Morokei slides: his mask, and the *Ozymandias* pairing.

---

## The short version (for the coda)

Show four screenshots:
1. the arches and city ruins;

## Site biography

| When | What | Evidence a visitor sees |
|------|------|------------------------|
| Merethic Era | Built *"as a temple to the dragons"*. Grew into **Bromjunaar**, possibly the Dragon Cult's capital. The in-game book is cautious: *"believed by some to have been the capital… it is known that the highest ranking Priests of the Cult met at Labyrinthian."* | Arches, terraces, tablets, draugr, the Word Wall, Morokei himself |
| After the Dragon War | Bromjunaar *"crumbled and was left abandoned."* | Ruined huts and a ruined tower |
| Undated | A survey under Jarl Gjalund: *"Bromjunaar… An Old Settlement, much Reduced from Former Days. Lumber and Stone."* | (Text only.) The ruins were treated as a **quarry**. |
| First Era (no year) | Archmage **Shalidor** builds his labyrinth *"deep in the ruins of Bromjunaar"*, to test would-be Archmages. | Shalidor's Maze: a later building cut into an older site |
| Early Third Era | A piece of the Staff of Chaos is recovered here (the *Arena* story). *"Loyalist pilgrims"* retrace the hero's steps. | (Text only.) Heritage tourism. |
| 4th Era, before 201 (no year) | **Savos Aren's** College expedition goes badly wrong, and he **seals the ruin**. | Ghostly "after-images", student bodies, the sealed door |
| Recently | A Breton scholar is murdered by the thugs he hired. Frost trolls move in. | Breton skeleton with an Orcish dagger in its ribs; *Hired Thug's Missive*; trolls |
| 4E 201 | The Dragonborn opens the seal with the **Torc of Labyrinthian**, kills Morokei, and takes his mask and the Staff of Magnus. | *You* |

UESP gives no year for Savos's expedition. The lore page says only "sometime before or after 3E 427".

## What to point out, zone by zone

UESP divides the interior into four zones.

### Exterior: Bromjunaar and Shalidor's Maze
- **The way in:** you approach *"under two huge arches"*, past terraces, stone huts, burial urns and a ruined tower. Four frost trolls live here.
- **The round building:** *"the skeleton of a Breton"* with *"an Orcish dagger stuck in the Breton's ribs"*. Next to it are the **Hired Thug's Missive** (*"That cursed thing can stay and rot with him and the trolls right here"*) and the **Wooden Mask**.
  - **Ask:** a scholar killed by his own hired diggers. Who actually does the digging on a site, and who gets the credit (or the blame)?
- **Shalidor's Maze:** an open-air maze that is *"not actually a maze"* (it has only one path). Its gates open to spells from different schools of magic. A burnt corpse holds a note with the riddle:
  > *"Enter Twice - Exit Only Once / Alteration will lead you to Destruction / Only Illusion shows the way to Restoration / Conjure not, but be conjured instead."*
  - **Ask (Q1):** a First Era wizard's test, built inside a Merethic city. Whose site is this?

### Bromjunaar Sanctuary: the Wooden Mask
- **What happens:** wearing the Wooden Mask in the round building sends you *"backwards in time"* to the **undamaged** room. Eight busts there hold the eight dragon priest masks, Morokei's among them.
- **Teaching point:** this is an in-game **reconstruction**, the site shown as it was before decay. Compare real reconstruction drawings and virtual models. What do they show confidently that the evidence can't actually prove?

### Labyrinthian (entry hall)
- **The bodies:** *"several dead lootable skeletons lying about the room all facing toward the exit."*
  - **Ask:** what does their position tell you? They died running out. Where bodies lie is evidence.
- **The tablet** (read aloud; then go back to the mask slide):
  > *"Hail All - Brave City Bromjunaar / Forever These Walls Shall Stand / May Enemies See Her Majesty / May All Quake to Behold Her."*

  This is the *Ozymandias* moment: *"Forever These Walls Shall Stand"*, carved on walls that didn't.
- **Then** a skeletal dragon.

### Labyrinthian Chasm
- **The conjurer:** *"the skeleton of a novice conjurer that was obviously killed where he sat"* at an arcane enchanter, with blood on it.
- **The Argonian:** *"a dead Argonian with an ancient Nord greatsword sticking out of the corpse"*. Use this for the **association** trap:
  - The greatsword is ancient, but the death is recent. An old object in a new context doesn't make the context old.
  - The newest thing present (the Argonian expedition member) dates the event. That's terminus post quem.
- **Doors:** an ice-sealed door opens with fire magic.

### Labyrinthian Thoroughfare
- **What's there:** trolls, skeletons, and a graveyard with wisps and a wispmother. A second tablet:
  > *"Here Lie the Hallowed Dead of Bromjunaar / May Ever Their Souls Be Their Own."*
- **Doors:** a fire-sealed door opens with frost magic.

### Labyrinthian Tribune: Morokei
- **What's there:** a throne room with a Word Wall (*UL*, "eternity", the third word of Slow Time), then Morokei's chamber with its waterfall pool.
- **Morokei's lines:** they show he remembers the *last* excavation:
  > *"Who comes to my dark kingdom?"* · *"Cowardly men shall find no mercy here."* (*Nivahriin muz fent siiv nid aaz het*)
  > *"Have you returned, Aren? My old friend?"* · *"Do you seek to finish that which you could not?"*
  > *"You... You are not Aren, are you? Has he sent you in his place?"*
- **The shield:** two "Enthralled Wizards" hold a shield around him. They are the students **Atmah and Hafnar**, whom Savos sacrificed to hold Morokei.
- **The mask:** Morokei's moonstone dragon-priest mask (*"Magicka regenerates 100% faster"*). He also carries the Staff of Magnus. How he got it is *"somehow obtained"* (UESP doesn't explain).
- **On the way out:** the Thalmor agent Estormo ambushes you.

## Savos Aren's expedition: testimony vs. the official story
- **The team:** Savos Aren, Atmah, Girduin, Takes-In-Light, Hafnar Ice-Fist and Elvali Veren, all College students.
- **Who led it?** UESP's pages disagree. In the game, Savos says *"this whole idea was Atmah's to begin with."*
- **How you learn the story:** ghostly after-images replay the expedition at five points as you go deeper. There are no journals.
  - **Entry hall.** Savos: *"Enchanted weapons, tomes of ancient knowledge, Shalidor's secrets themselves -- who knows what we could find!"* Atmah: *"Against six College-trained mages? I think we'll be fine."*
  - **After the dragon.** Atmah, about Girduin: *"There isn't enough of him left to go back in after."*
  - **End of the Thoroughfare.** Hafnar, about Elvali: *"Something grabbed her from behind."*
  - **Before the last door.** Hafnar: *"We stay together, no matter what. Agreed?"* Savos: *"Agreed. We all stay together."*
  - **Last scene.** Savos: *"...I'm sorry, friends. I'm so sorry! I had no choice! It was the only way to make sure that monster never escaped! I promise you, I'll never let this happen again! I'll seal this whole place away..."*
- **Unused line** (in the game files, never played): *"Hafnar said. I looked him in the eyes and lied to him."*
- **Teaching points:**
  - **Text vs. testimony.** The site's own record (the ghosts, the bodies, the enthralled students) tells a darker story than the expedition leader ever told anyone.
  - **Sealing and terminus ante quem.** Savos sealed the ruin, and everything inside the door predates that seal. Everything except Morokei, who is still very much active. Sealed contexts aren't always inert.

## Access and prep
- **Interior:** needs the **Torc of Labyrinthian**. You get it from Mirabelle at the end of *Containment*, and the door is *"inaccessible until the quest The Staff of Magnus has been started."* That means being deep in the College of Winterhold questline.
- **Always open:** the exterior, Shalidor's Maze, Lost Valkygg and the Wooden Mask sanctuary need no quest.
- **Level:** suggested and minimum level 24.
- **Length:** UESP gives none. Long: plan on screenshots rather than live play **(unverified estimate)**.
- **Exit:** a ladder at the end comes out near the second arch.
