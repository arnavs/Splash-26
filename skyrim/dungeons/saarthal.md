# Saarthal: A Site About Archaeology

*Optional comparison, 3–5 min from screenshots, or a live alternative to Bleak Falls if you have a College save.*

Facts and quotes below are checked against UESP. Anything UESP doesn't confirm is marked **(unverified)**.

**Why this site:**
- It's the **oldest Nord site**: the first Atmoran city, destroyed in the Night of Tears. It's on the *Merethic Era: the First Footprints* slide.
- In 4E 201 the College of Winterhold is running **an actual excavation** there. That makes it the one place in Skyrim that is about archaeology itself: who digs, by whose permission, what counts as a find, and what happens to it.
- `images/saarthal.jpg` is already in the project if you want a slide.

## Site biography

| When | What | Source / caveat |
|------|------|-----------------|
| Merethic Era | Atmoran settlers build **Saarthal**, *"the first capital of Nord civilization… as well as the largest of the ancient Nordic cities."* | UESP's lore page says *middle* Merethic; *Before the Ages of Man* says *Late*. The sources disagree. |
| During construction | The Nords dig up the **Eye of Magnus** and bury it under the city to keep it secret. | Only from the in-game book *Night of Tears* (Dranor Seleth), which hedges: *"I believe"*. |
| Night of Tears | An Elven force *"attacked and burned the first human city of Skyrim."* Ysgramor and his sons survive. | The cause is disputed (see below). |
| The Return | Ysgramor and the Five Hundred Companions retake and **rebuild** Saarthal. | *Imperial Report on Saarthal* |
| Late Merethic / early First Era | *"destroyed and finally abandoned."* | *Daynas Valen's Notes*: *"the shattered crypts of Saarthal, half-buried even then"* |
| Early First Era | **Jyrik Gauldurson**, a murderer, is sealed inside the ruined city, in the chamber where the Eye had been placed. | A **later burial inside an older city** |
| Late Second Era | *"recently been located by Imperial archaeologists"* (*Pocket Guide to the Empire*, dated 2E 864). An Imperial dig under Sentius Floronius sorts *"original architecture and those that were rebuilt after Ysgramor retook the city."* | The College is **not the first** to dig here. |
| 4E 201 | The College's dig (*Under Saarthal*) | The player is on the team |

**Span check:** Daynas Valen, writing in 4E, says *"Four thousand years have passed"* since Jyrik's day. That's a round in-world figure, consistent with the deck's "4,450+" from the end of the Merethic.

## The Night of Tears: whose story?
The sources line up by faction:
- **Imperial** *Pocket Guide*: the elves *"saw that the vital young race would soon surpass their stagnant culture."*
- **An Elven reader's note in the margin of the same book:** *"Ysgramor's provocations and blasphemies have, of course, been long forgotten."*
- ***Night of Tears*** (book): the attack was about the buried artifact.
- ***Imperial Report***: the attack was *"very focused… perhaps a singular goal."*
- **UESP:** *"The reason for the Night of Tears has been lost to time."*

**Ask:** this is the deck's *"Who is indigenous to Skyrim?"* question again. Who gets to tell a site's origin story?

## The College dig: method, permission, people

**The team:** Tolfdir (in charge); Arniel Gane, *"one of our scholars, here working on cataloging our finds"*; the apprentices Onmund (Nord), Brelyna Maryon (Dunmer) and J'zargo (Khajiit); and the player.

**Method, in Tolfdir's words:**
- **Research question:** *"We're particularly interested in the prevalence of magical seals placed on the tombs here."*
- **What counts as a find:** *"Any enchanted items will do; the usefulness of the enchantment is irrelevant."*
- **Caution:** *"Please be careful here. The site isn't entirely secured."*
- **The apprentices' jobs:** Brelyna is to *"search for warding magics… Don't interact with them, just identify them."* J'zargo is to *"verify that we're the first ones here? Look for any amount of tampering with the tombs."* (That's checking whether the context is sealed.)
- **Arniel:** *"Try and be careful, all right? We don't want to damage anything."* Later: *"It probably doesn't even matter now, what with the mess that's been made in here."*

**Ask:** what does *"any enchanted items will do"* leave out? A dig only finds what it's looking for. This is **selection bias** built into the research question.

**Ethics, voiced by the apprentices:**
- **Onmund** (a descendant): *"There's no chance anyone in authority approved this. Our ancestors should be allowed to rest in peace."* Also: *"I wonder if everyone else would be so happy to rifle through the bones of their ancestors."* And later: *"If only we'd stayed out of Saarthal."*
- **Brelyna:** *"To think, my ancestors destroyed the homes of Onmund's ancestors."*
- **J'zargo:** *"Do you think there's treasure here?"*
- **Locals:** UESP says the local Nords disapproved of the dig **(exact in-game line unverified)**.

**Ask:** three students, three relationships to the dead: descendant, descendant of the attackers, treasure-hunter. Which one is the archaeologist?

**The workers who were cut:** UESP says the dig *"originally"* had six hired miners (Wood Elves and Dark Elves): *"As long as the College keeps paying, I'll keep digging."* They were cut from the game. **Ask:** who does the physical digging on real excavations, and do they appear in the publication?

## What to point out, zone by zone

UESP lists two interior zones.

### Exterior
- Wooden platforms, bridges and ramps going down into the dig; food barrels; shelves of burial urns; a table, a chest and two bedrolls.
- **Point out:** the College's own camp is a new layer on top of the site.

### Saarthal Excavation (the dug part)
- **What's there:** chambers *"several stories high, and dominated by ancient stone pillars. The College has built various rickety wooden ramps around the pillars."* Also carts, buckets, scaffolding, pickaxes and a collapsed bridge.
- **The finds, in order:**
  1. Optional enchanted rings.
  2. The **Saarthal Amulet**. Taking it sets off a spear trap.
  3. With the amulet on, a spell makes a wall collapse and reveals a **sealed section**. Tolfdir: *"Why in the world would this be sealed off?"*
- **Nerien of the Psijic Order** appears and freezes time:
  > *"Hold, mage, and listen well... Know that you have set in motion a chain of events that cannot be stopped. Judgment has not been passed, as you had no way of knowing… the Psijic Order believes in you."*

  Tolfdir: *"The Psijics have no connection to these ruins."* **Ask:** an outside group with no tie to the site claims authority over it.

### Saarthal (the undug part)
- **What's there:** coffin-lined halls, burial niches, traps and two rotating-pillar puzzles. Then the **orb chamber**, *"bathed in the blue-green light of a massive orb"*, with Jyrik on his altar. Tolfdir: *"Why is this buried so far within Saarthal?"*
- **The trap: Jyrik and the Eye.** Jyrik is First Era and the city is Merethic. Finding them together doesn't mean they're contemporary.
  - Jyrik is an **intrusive burial**: sealed *into* an older ruin, in the very chamber where the Eye had been placed.
  - Association only holds for a single, undisturbed deposition event, and this is two events.
- **The end:** a fern-filled grotto with the **Ice Form** Word Wall.

### After the dig
- Tolfdir: *"I don't dare leave this unattended."* The Eye goes to the College.
- In the end the Psijics take it away, *"claiming that it was too unstable to keep in Mundus."*
- **Ask:** the find leaves its site, then its finders. Who decides where an artifact belongs?

## Access and prep
- **How it starts:** join the College of Winterhold and finish *First Lessons*. *Under Saarthal* then starts automatically. Until then the door is *"locked (key required)."*
- **Where:** southwest of Winterhold.
- **Level:** suggested level 6.
- **Length:** UESP gives none. Tolfdir's walk there alone takes *"at least four in-game hours"*. Make a save at the dig entrance **(playtime unverified)**.
- **Next quest:** *Hitting the Books*.
